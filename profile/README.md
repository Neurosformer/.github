# Neurosformer

**AI workflow intelligence for document-heavy, compliance-heavy, and decision-heavy industries.**

Neurosformer builds secure AI systems that help organizations turn unstructured information into structured workflows, reviewable decisions, and auditable operations.

Our focus is not generic AI demos. We build practical AI products around real operational pain: documents, compliance, traceability, clinical documentation, crop risk, evidence review, and human-in-the-loop workflow automation.

## What We Build

| Product | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| [NeuroDocOps](https://github.com/Neurosformer/NeuroDocOps) | AI document operations for regulated workflows |
| [NeuroFashionOps](https://github.com/Neurosformer/NeuroFashionOps) | Apparel supply-chain compliance, traceability, and quality AI |
| [NeuroClinic Docs](https://github.com/Neurosformer/NeuroClinicDocs) | Clinical documentation and referral workflow AI |
| [AgroNeuro](https://github.com/Neurosformer/AgroNeuro) | Climate and crop-risk intelligence for agribusiness |

## Core Capabilities

- OCR, layout parsing, and document understanding
- Structured extraction from PDFs, scans, forms, tables, and business records
- Retrieval-augmented generation with citations
- Human-in-the-loop review and approval workflows
- Compliance checklist automation
- Audit logs and evidence tracking
- Secure workflow dashboards and operational reporting
- AI-assisted decision support with clear boundaries and expert review

## Product Strategy

Neurosformer starts with a reusable AI operations backbone and builds vertical products where global demand is strong.

1. **NeuroDocOps** is the core platform for regulated document workflows.
2. **NeuroFashionOps** applies that engine to apparel supply-chain compliance and traceability.
3. **NeuroClinic Docs** focuses on healthcare documentation and referral workflows without autonomous diagnosis.
4. **AgroNeuro** supports crop-risk intelligence through agribusiness, insurer, NGO, government, and cooperative partners.

## Principles

- Build workflow products, not isolated AI chatbots
- Keep humans in the loop for high-risk decisions
- Show citations, confidence, and source evidence
- Design for privacy, security, and auditability from the beginning
- Start narrow, validate with real users, then expand
- Prefer practical business value over model hype
