# Code of Conduct

Neurosformer projects should remain professional, respectful, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and clearly
- Give constructive technical feedback
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints
- Focus discussions on the work and the product goals

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or abusive language
- Sharing private information without permission
- Spam, trolling, or intentionally disruptive behavior

## Reporting

Report concerns to [neurosformer@gmail.com](mailto:neurosformer@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed and handled privately.
